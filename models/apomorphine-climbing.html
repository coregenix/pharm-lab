Antipsychotic Activity · Dopamine Agonist Model

Apomorphine-Induced Climbing

Apomorphine, a direct dopamine D1/D2 agonist, induces stereotyped climbing behavior in mice placed in a wire-mesh cage, reflecting mesolimbic/striatal dopamine activation. A climbing score (0 = all four paws on floor, to 6 = continuous climbing) is recorded 10-20 min post-apomorphine. Antipsychotics suppress apomorphine-induced climbing dose-dependently.
