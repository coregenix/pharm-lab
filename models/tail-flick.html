Analgesic Activity · Spinal Reflex Model

Tail-Flick Test (D'Amour & Smith)

A beam of radiant heat is focused on the rat's tail; latency to tail withdrawal (flick) is recorded. Unlike the hot plate, this is largely a spinal reflex, so it is especially sensitive to opioid analgesics acting at spinal opioid receptors. Cut-off of 10 s avoids tissue damage.
