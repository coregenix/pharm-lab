Parkinsonism · Mitochondrial Toxin Model

Rotenone-Induced Model

Chronic low-dose rotenone, a mitochondrial complex I inhibitor, reproduces many hallmark features of Parkinson's disease - including nigral dopaminergic loss and motor deficits - modeling the mitochondrial-dysfunction hypothesis. Motor coordination (rotarod fall latency) is used to track disease severity and the protective effect of candidate neuroprotective agents.
