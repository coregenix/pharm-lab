Motor Coordination · Sedative Screening Model

Rotarod Test

A mouse/rat is placed on a rotating rod (typically 16–25 rpm) and the time it stays on before falling is recorded (cut-off 180 s). Only animals that pass a pretest are used. Muscle relaxants and sedatives (benzodiazepines, high-dose antihistamines) shorten fall latency by impairing motor coordination — useful for detecting unwanted CNS depression in new compounds as well as screening muscle relaxants.
