Antidepressant Activity · Behavioral Despair Model

Forced Swim Test (Porsolt)

A mouse or rat is placed in an inescapable cylinder of water for 6 minutes. After initial escape attempts, untreated animals adopt an immobile, floating posture ("behavioral despair"). Immobility time over the final 4 minutes is scored. Antidepressants of multiple classes (tricyclics, SSRIs) reduce immobility time, increasing swimming/climbing.
