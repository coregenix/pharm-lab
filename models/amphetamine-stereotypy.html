Amphetamine-Induced Stereotypy

Amphetamine releases synaptic dopamine and induces stereotyped behavior - repetitive sniffing, licking, gnawing - scored on a 0-4 stereotypy scale at intervals after dosing. Typical antipsychotics attenuate the stereotypy score, complementing the apomorphine model which works via direct receptor agonism rather than dopamine release.
