Elevated Zero Maze

A variant of the elevated plus maze without a center zone: an annular platform divided into two open and two enclosed quadrants, removing ambiguity about time spent at the center. Time spent in the open quadrants over a 5-min session indexes anxiety-like behavior; anxiolytics increase open-quadrant time.
