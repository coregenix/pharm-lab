Learning & Memory · Aversive Retention Model

Passive (Step-Through) Avoidance

An animal receives a mild foot-shock upon stepping from a lit compartment into a preferred dark one during training. On the retention test (24 h later), the latency to re-enter the dark compartment reflects how well the aversive association was learned and retained. Amnesic agents shorten retention latency; nootropics lengthen it.
