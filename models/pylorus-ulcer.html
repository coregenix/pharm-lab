Pylorus Ligation-Induced Ulcer (Shay Model)

The pylorus is ligated under anesthesia, causing gastric acid and pepsin to accumulate and self-digest the mucosa over 4 h. On sacrifice, gastric juice volume, pH and free/total acidity are measured, and the stomach is scored for an ulcer index. Antisecretory drugs (H2 blockers, PPIs) reduce acid output and ulcer index; antacids raise pH without necessarily reducing volume.
