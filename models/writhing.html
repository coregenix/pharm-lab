Analgesic Activity · Peripheral (Visceral) Pain Model

Acetic Acid-Induced Writhing Test

0.6% acetic acid is injected intraperitoneally, releasing prostaglandins that irritate the peritoneum and provoke a stereotyped "writhing" response (abdominal contraction, trunk twist, hind-limb extension). Writhes are counted over a 20-minute observation window. Because the response is prostaglandin-mediated, this model is exquisitely sensitive to NSAIDs, unlike the hot plate test.
