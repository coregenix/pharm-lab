Anticonvulsant Activity · GABA-Antagonist (Petit Mal) Model

Pentylenetetrazole (PTZ)-Induced Seizures

PTZ (70-90 mg/kg s.c.), a GABA-A receptor antagonist, reliably induces clonic seizures and is the standard model for compounds active against absence and myoclonic seizures - complementing MES, which is specific to tonic-clonic (grand mal) activity. Animals are scored protected if no clonic seizure occurs within 30 min.
