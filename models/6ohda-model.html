Parkinsonism · Neurotoxin Lesion Model

6-OHDA Lesion Model

6-hydroxydopamine, injected stereotaxically into the substantia nigra/medial forebrain bundle, selectively destroys dopaminergic neurons, producing a unilateral parkinsonian lesion. Challenge with a dopamine agonist (apomorphine) produces contralateral rotations proportional to lesion severity - a standard readout for neuroprotective candidate drugs.
