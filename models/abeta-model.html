Alzheimer's Disease · Amyloid-Beta Toxicity Model

A-beta-Induced Model

Intracerebroventricular injection of aggregated amyloid-beta (1-42) peptide directly reproduces the toxic protein pathology central to Alzheimer's disease, causing synaptic dysfunction and working-memory deficits. Y-maze spontaneous alternation, impaired by amyloid-beta, is used to assess candidate disease-modifying therapies.
