Anxiolytic Activity · Approach-Avoidance Model

Light-Dark Box

A two-compartment box - one brightly lit, one dark - with a connecting opening exploits rodents' conflict between exploratory drive and aversion to bright, open spaces. Time spent in the lit compartment over a 5-min (300 s) session is the primary measure; anxiolytics increase time in light, anxiogenic agents decrease it.
