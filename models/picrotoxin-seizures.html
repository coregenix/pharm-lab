Anticonvulsant Activity · GABA-Antagonist Model

Picrotoxin-Induced Seizures

Picrotoxin blocks the GABA-A chloride channel directly, producing generalized clonic-tonic seizures. It is used alongside PTZ to confirm a GABAergic mechanism of anticonvulsant action and to test compounds acting at sites downstream of the benzodiazepine-binding pocket.
