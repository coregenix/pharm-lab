Anticonvulsant Activity · Grand Mal Seizure Model

Maximal Electroshock Seizure (MES) Test

A supramaximal electric shock (50 mA, 0.2 s, via corneal or ear electrodes) is delivered to induce tonic hind-limb extension (THLE) — a model of generalized tonic-clonic (grand mal) seizures. A drug is "protective" if it abolishes the tonic hind-limb extensor phase. Phenytoin and carbamazepine are classically active in this model; each animal is scored protected / not protected.
