Locomotor Activity · General CNS Screening

Actophotometer Test

The animal is placed in a chamber lined with photocell/beam pairs; each beam interruption from spontaneous movement is counted over a 10-minute session. This is a general screen for CNS depressant or stimulant activity of a compound — a useful baseline test to run before more specific behavioral assays like EPM or rotarod, since gross sedation can confound their interpretation.
