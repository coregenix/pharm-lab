Anxiolytic Activity · Exploratory Behavior Model

Hole Board Test

A flat board perforated with equally spaced holes is placed in an open arena; the number of head-dips into the holes over a 5-min session is scored as a measure of exploratory drive versus anxiety, and is also sensitive to sedation. Anxiolytics increase head-dipping; sedatives and CNS depressants suppress it.
