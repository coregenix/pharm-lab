Analgesic Activity · Tonic Inflammatory Pain Model

Formalin-Induced Paw Licking

A dilute formalin solution (2.5%, 20 uL) is injected into the dorsal paw, producing a biphasic nociceptive response: an early neurogenic phase (0-5 min) and a later inflammatory phase (15-30 min). Paw-licking time during the late phase is scored, since it is the phase most sensitive to NSAIDs and centrally-acting analgesics.
