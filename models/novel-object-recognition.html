Novel Object Recognition

Following familiarization with two identical objects, one is replaced with a novel object after a retention interval. A healthy animal spends disproportionately more time exploring the novel object; this preference, expressed as a discrimination index, is impaired by amnesic agents and restored by cognitive enhancers.
