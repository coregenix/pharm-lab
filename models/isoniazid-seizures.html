Anticonvulsant Activity · Pyridoxine-Antagonist Model

Isoniazid-Induced Seizures

Isoniazid inhibits pyridoxal phosphokinase, depleting active vitamin B6 and reducing GABA synthesis (GAD is a B6-dependent enzyme), which precipitates seizures. This model demonstrates that pyridoxine supplementation - not typical anticonvulsants - is the definitive antidote.
