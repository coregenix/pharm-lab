Analgesic Activity · Mechanical Nociception Model

Randall-Selitto Test

Increasing mechanical pressure is applied to the hind paw (often pre-inflamed with carrageenan) via an analgesiometer until the animal vocalizes or withdraws. The pressure threshold (grams) is recorded, and is the standard model for testing analgesics against hyperalgesia of inflamed tissue.
