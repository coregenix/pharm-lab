Antipsychotic Activity / Extrapyramidal Side-Effect Screen

Catalepsy Test (Bar Test)

The animal's forepaws are placed on a raised horizontal bar; the time it remains in this imposed posture without correcting it is timed (catalepsy duration). Typical antipsychotics induce catalepsy - modeling extrapyramidal side effects - while atypical agents like clozapine produce far less, a key screening distinction.
