Haloperidol-Induced Catalepsy

Haloperidol, a potent D2 antagonist, induces catalepsy that closely mimics drug-induced parkinsonism/extrapyramidal side effects. This bar-test model is used specifically to screen antiparkinsonian and anticholinergic agents for their ability to reverse dopamine-antagonist-induced catalepsy.
