Acute Anti-Inflammatory Activity

Carrageenan-Induced Paw Edema

1% carrageenan (0.1 mL) is injected into the sub-plantar surface of the rat hind paw. Paw volume is measured by plethysmometer at 0, 1, 2, 3 and 4 h — a biphasic response (early histamine/serotonin phase, later prostaglandin-mediated phase) that peaks around 3 h. NSAIDs inhibit the later phase most strongly, which is why the 3 h reading is used to calculate % inhibition of edema.
