Y-Maze Spontaneous Alternation

A rodent freely explores a Y-shaped maze with three identical arms. Because rodents naturally prefer to explore a newly-visited arm rather than repeat the one just visited, the percentage of 'spontaneous alternations' indexes spatial working memory without any training or reward needed.
