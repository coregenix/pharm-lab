MK-801-Induced Hyperlocomotion

MK-801 (dizocilpine), an NMDA receptor antagonist, produces marked hyperlocomotion and is used to model the glutamatergic hypothesis of schizophrenia. Locomotor counts are recorded in an activity chamber over 30-60 min. Atypical antipsychotics, notably clozapine, are more effective here than typical agents - a key pharmacological distinction.
