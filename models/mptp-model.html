Parkinsonism · Selective Dopaminergic Neurotoxin Model

MPTP Model

MPTP is converted by MAO-B (in glia) to MPP+, which is selectively taken up by dopamine transporters into nigral neurons, where it poisons mitochondrial complex I, closely mimicking idiopathic Parkinson's disease pathology. Spontaneous locomotor activity, markedly reduced after MPTP, is used to gauge neuroprotection by candidate agents (notably MAO-B inhibitors, which block MPTP's own bioactivation).
