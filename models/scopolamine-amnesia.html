Alzheimer's Disease · Cholinergic Deficit Model

Scopolamine-Induced Amnesia

Scopolamine, a muscarinic antagonist, blocks central cholinergic transmission and produces a reliable, reversible amnesia, modeling the cholinergic-deficit hypothesis of Alzheimer's disease. Step-through passive avoidance retention latency, sharply reduced by scopolamine, is restored by cholinesterase inhibitors and other nootropics.
