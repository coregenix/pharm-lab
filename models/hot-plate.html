Analgesic Activity · Central (Supraspinal) Pain Model

Hot Plate Test

The mouse is placed on a plate held at 55 ± 0.5 °C. Latency to the first paw-lick or jump response is timed, reflecting a centrally-mediated (supraspinal) nociceptive reflex. Opioid analgesics (e.g. morphine) prolong this latency dose-dependently; NSAIDs like aspirin have only a mild effect, since the hot plate response is not primarily prostaglandin-mediated. A 15 s cut-off prevents tissue damage.
