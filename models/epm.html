Anxiolytic Activity · Ethological Model

Elevated Plus Maze (EPM)

A plus-shaped maze with two open and two enclosed ("closed") arms, elevated ~50 cm above the floor, exploits rodents' natural aversion to open, elevated spaces. A rodent is placed at the center and observed for 5 min. Anxiolytics (benzodiazepines) increase the proportion of time and entries into the open arms; anxiogenic agents do the opposite.
