Motor Coordination · Fine Balance Model

Beam Walking Test

An animal crosses a narrow elevated beam toward a home enclosure; the number of hind-limb foot slips off the beam is counted, providing a more sensitive measure of fine motor coordination and balance than rotarod alone - particularly useful in Parkinsonian and lesion models where subtle deficits might otherwise be missed.
