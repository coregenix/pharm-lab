Antidepressant Activity · Chronic Stress Model

Learned Helplessness

Animals first receive unavoidable, inescapable shocks over 1-2 days. When later given the chance to escape a shock (shuttle-box, escapable trials), pre-exposed animals show repeated escape failures, modeling depressive helplessness. Chronic antidepressant treatment reduces the number of escape failures on re-test.
