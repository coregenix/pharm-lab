Sedative / Hypnotic Activity Model

Pentobarbitone-Induced Sleeping Time

A sub-hypnotic-potentiating dose of pentobarbitone is given, and duration of loss of righting reflex ('sleeping time') is measured. CNS depressants potentiate (prolong) pentobarbitone sleeping time; stimulants and enzyme inducers shorten it - a classic screen for CNS depressant/stimulant activity of a new compound.
