Antidepressant Activity · Behavioral Despair Model

Tail Suspension Test

A mouse is suspended by its tail for 6 minutes; after initial escape-oriented movement, untreated mice adopt an immobile posture. Immobility time is scored, analogous to the forced swim test but avoiding confounds from water exposure. Antidepressants across classes reduce immobility.
