Motor Coordination · Gait Analysis Model

Footprint Analysis

The animal's fore- and hind-paws are inked and it walks through a narrow, paper-lined runway to a dark goal box; stride length and gait base width are measured from the resulting footprint pattern. Reduced stride length is a sensitive sign of Parkinsonian or motor-lesion gait deficits, and improved stride length is used as a functional readout of neuroprotective treatment.
